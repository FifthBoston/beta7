Common Problems

Signs You Need Water Heaters Service

No Hot Water

Complete loss of hot water means a failed heating element, pilot light issue, or thermostat problem.

Not Enough Hot Water

Running out of hot water quickly? Your tank may be undersized, sediment-filled, or losing efficiency.

Strange Noises

Popping, rumbling, or cracking sounds indicate sediment buildup—your water heater is overworking.

Leaking Tank

Water pooling around your heater is an emergency. Internal corrosion often means replacement is needed.

Common Questions

Water Heaters FAQ

How long do water heaters last?

Tank water heaters typically last 8-12 years. Tankless units can last 15-20+ years with proper maintenance. If your heater is over 10 years old and having problems, replacement often makes more financial sense than repair.

Should I switch to tankless?

Tankless water heaters cost more upfront ($2,500-$4,500 installed) but save 20-30% on water heating costs and last twice as long. They're ideal for larger homes or high hot water demand. We can help you calculate the ROI for your situation.

Why is my water heater making noise?

Popping or rumbling usually indicates sediment buildup at the bottom of the tank. The water beneath the sediment boils and creates noise. Annual flushing prevents this. If severe, the tank may need replacement.

Can you repair any brand?

Yes! We service all brands: Rheem, A.O. Smith, Bradford White, Rinnai, Navien, Noritz, State, and more. We stock common parts and can get specialty parts quickly.
