Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to questions Dallas homeowners ask us every day

How much does a plumber cost in Dallas?

Plumbing costs in Dallas typically range from $150-$450 for common repairs. Our service calls start at $89. Drain cleaning averages $150-$300, water heater installation $800-$2,500, and major repairs like sewer line work can range $1,500-$5,000+. We provide free estimates with upfront pricing before any work begins—no surprises.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency plumbing service?

Yes! We offer 24/7 emergency plumbing service throughout Dallas-Fort Worth. Our emergency team responds within 60 minutes for urgent issues like burst pipes, sewage backups, gas leaks, and flooding. There's no extra charge for nights, weekends, or holidays. Call (214) 555-1234 anytime.

What areas do you serve in DFW?

We serve the entire Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex including Dallas, Fort Worth, Plano, Frisco, McKinney, Allen, Irving, Arlington, Richardson, Carrollton, Garland, Mesquite, Lewisville, Flower Mound, Denton, and 30+ more cities. We have plumbers stationed throughout DFW for fast response times.

Are your plumbers licensed and insured?

Absolutely. All our plumbers are licensed by the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners. We carry $2 million in liability insurance and full workers' compensation coverage. Our master plumber license number is M-12345. We're also BBB A+ accredited since 1965.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes, we provide free estimates for all plumbing work. Our technicians will diagnose the issue, explain your options, and give you a detailed written quote before starting any repairs. If you don't approve the price, you don't pay anything. No pressure, no obligation.

How quickly can you get to my home?

For emergencies, we guarantee a 60-minute response time anywhere in the DFW metroplex. For scheduled appointments, we offer same-day service for most calls made before noon. We provide a 2-hour arrival window (not 6!) and always call 30 minutes before arriving.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover), checks, and financing. We offer 0% financing for up to 18 months on approved credit for larger jobs. We can also work directly with your home warranty or insurance company.

Do you guarantee your work?

Yes! We offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee on all work. If something we repaired fails within our warranty period (1 year on labor, varies on parts), we'll fix it free. If you're not completely satisfied for any reason, we'll make it right or refund your money.
